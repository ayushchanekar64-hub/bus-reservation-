❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the interactive bus seat selection work? +
Our realistic bus hull displays the full 2+2 or 2+1 layout including the driver cabin, entrance door, and emergency exits. Green seats are available, grey seats are already booked, and blue seats are your active selection. Window seats (ends with 'W') and Sleeper berths (ends with 'S') carry specialized luxury multipliers.
Can other passengers see my personal bookings? +
No! SwiftBus implements strict per-user authentication and data isolation. When logged in as Aarav or Rajesh (or your own custom account), the "My Bookings" section exclusively filters and displays only your tickets. Other passengers' records remain strictly confidential.
Does the bus ticket fit on a single A4 page when printed? +
Yes! Our print stylesheet guarantees that the entire commercial e-ticket—including journey timeline, passenger table, fare breakdown, verified stamp, scanner QR code, conductor stub, and guidelines—fits on exactly ONE single A4 page without spilling over.
Is a physical printout mandatory or is M-Ticket accepted? +
Physical paper printout is 100% optional! Under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988, showing the electronic ticket on your mobile phone screen, WhatsApp, or SMS along with a valid original Government photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / DL) is fully accepted by all conductors.
